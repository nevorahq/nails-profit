# Privacy and consent release checklist

The application contains versioned RU/RO/EN pilot terms and privacy notices, requires explicit acceptance for new signups, stores the server-selected versions/timestamps, masks PII in logs, removes raw imports after confirmation, and gives an Owner export/deletion controls.

Technical completion does not constitute legal approval. Before production, the responsible legal/privacy owner must:

- replace the operator description with the exact legal entity and contact details from the pilot agreement;
- confirm the operator/studio controller-processor roles for Moldova and every launch market;
- approve processing purposes, legal bases, retention periods and subprocessors;
- approve client-data instructions and the deletion/anonymization policy;
- confirm incident-notification contacts and deadlines;
- record approval of `TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_VERSION` used by the application.

Release evidence: reviewer, UTC date, approved versions, applicable markets and link to the signed approval. Any material text change must increment the relevant version and decide whether existing users must accept again.
